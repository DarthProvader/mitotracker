# Design

Two folders, in the order they happened.

`stitch/` — sketches from Google Stitch, briefed with [`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) and asked for a
modern treatment of it. Five screens, each as the PNG it produced and the HTML behind it. The HTML is
the useful part: it is where the exact gradients, hairlines and glow radii were read off from, since a
sketch says "this glows" and the stylesheet says by how much.

They are sketches, not a specification. Their numbers were wrong in places — dose limits especially —
and the vocabulary is not always ours. What was taken from them is the look; what was corrected against
[`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md) and Mito Light's own manual is everything the numbers mean.

`prototype/` — the same screens as built, captured from the running app on an emulator driving the
simulated panel. Worth keeping next to the sketches, because the gap between the two is the honest
answer to how far the build got.
